# iPhone setup (participants, Moonlight app)

About 3 minutes. Requires iOS 17 or newer.

Until Moonlight is on TestFlight, the organizer installs the app on your iPhone from their Mac (plug in your phone, unlock it, tap **Trust**). The first time, iPhone asks you to turn on **Developer Mode**: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode → on, then restart.

Then open **Moonlight**:

1. **Get started.** Choose English or Nederlands.
2. Enter your **name**, exactly as agreed, and your **goal time** (e.g. 21:00). Tap **Continue**.
3. **Allow Screen Time access.** Confirm with Face ID or your passcode. Moonlight only learns whether you passed 2, 5, 15 or 60 minutes during your night, never which apps or what you did.
4. **Choose what counts.** Tap every category in the list (so the circle is ticked for each), then **Done**. Picking a category also covers apps you install later.
5. **Allow notifications** for the morning reminder at 09:00.

That's it. Measuring starts at your goal time tonight. Open Moonlight in the morning to see how your group did.

If you used the Shortcuts automations before, turn them off: Shortcuts → Automation → swipe left on each Moonlight automation → Delete.

## Good to know

- To change your goal time: Moonlight → Settings → Phone down by → Save. It applies from the next night.
- Settings → Screen Time shows whether measuring is on.
- Don't turn off Screen Time for Moonlight in iPhone Settings, or nights show as "Not reported".
