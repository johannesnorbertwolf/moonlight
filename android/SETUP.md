# Android setup (participants)

One app, about 2 minutes. Requires Android 10 or newer. No browser, no group code: the app puts you in the trial group by itself.

You need from the organizer the file **Moonlight test.apk** (sent by message, email or a cloud link).

## Install

1. Open the APK file on your phone. Android warns that the app is from an unknown source:
   - allow installing apps from the app you opened the file in (WhatsApp, Gmail, Files…),
   - if Google Play Protect asks, tap **More details → Install anyway**.
   - Samsung: if nothing happens, turn off **Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker** for the install (you can turn it back on afterwards).
2. Open **Moonlight**.

## Set up (in the app)

1. **Get started.** Choose English or Nederlands.
2. Enter your **name** and your **goal time** (e.g. 21:00). Tap **Continue**.
3. **Allow usage access:** tap **Open settings**, find **Moonlight** in the list and switch it on, then go back.
   - If the switch is greyed out with "Restricted setting": go to **Settings → Apps → Moonlight → ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings**, then try again.
4. **No battery limits:** tap **Allow** and confirm. (Samsung: also keep Moonlight out of **Settings → Battery → Background usage limits → Sleeping apps**.)
5. **Allow notifications** for the morning reminder at 09:00.

That's it. Measuring starts at your goal time tonight. Open Moonlight in the morning to see how your group did; tap a friend's moon to send 🌙, hold it for more.

## Good to know

- To change your goal time: Moonlight → Settings → Phone down by. It applies from the next night.
- Settings shows whether usage access and battery are fine, and has **Measure and upload now** to check the connection.
- Every morning at about 07:05 the app uploads last night by itself, and again whenever you open it.
- A night without an upload shows as "Not reported" (grey moon); opening the app usually fixes it.
- Updates: install the new APK over the old one; your setup stays.
