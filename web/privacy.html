Moonlight privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Moonlight is a challenge to keep your phone away during your night, together with a small group of people you invite. This page explains what the app stores and who can see it.

What we store

  • Your name, goal time and phone type, as you enter them when you join a group.
  • Which group you're in, and the emoji reactions you send.
  • One result per night. On iPhone, Moonlight uses Apple's Screen Time on your own phone and only learns whether you passed 2, 5, 15 or 60 minutes of use between your goal time and 07:00, and the time 2 minutes were reached. It never sees which apps you used or what you did in them. On Android, the measuring app sends the times the phone was in use during that window, without app names.
  • An anonymous sign-in ID, so the app recognises you. There are no accounts, passwords or email addresses.

Who can see it

Only the members of your group see your name, goal time, nightly result and reactions. That is the purpose of the app, and you agree to it by joining a group. Nobody else sees it.

What we don't do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of data with anyone. Screen Time data is used only to show your group how the night went.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase, which processes it on our behalf.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Leave group. This deletes your name, nightly results and reactions from the database immediately. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.
